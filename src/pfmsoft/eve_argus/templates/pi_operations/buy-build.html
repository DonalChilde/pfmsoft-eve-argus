P4 Buy/Build

Buy P2 at Jita Sell 5%, or build P2 on another planet from P1 priced at Jita Sell 5%. Build costs include P1 import and P2 export there. Every plan imports P2 and any direct P1 on the P4 planet, builds P3 locally, and exports only the finished P4. Estimates exclude hauling and capacity constraints.

Expand a breakdown to see the recommended source and quantity for each material. % Difference compares the build cost with the buy price: positive means building is cheaper, negative means buying is cheaper. A dash means the value is unavailable or not applicable; a sole priced option is not necessarily cheaper.

Buy? starts checked when buying is the best available option. Check it to buy or uncheck it to build for this output. This updates Best available, not the buy-all or build-all comparisons. If a chosen option cannot be priced, neither can the Best available plan.

Sales price source sets the finished P4 quote only; input prices stay on Jita Sell 5%. Profit is sale price minus unit cost, before sales taxes and fees. Margin is profit divided by sale price.
